Nifty Analyzer 2.0 — Institutional Command Desk

SIGNAL-ONLY SOFTWARE
NO AUTOMATED TRADING

This application never places, modifies, cancels, or squares off broker
orders. Zerodha Kite is used only for read-only market data (LTP, OHLC,
history, quotes, instruments, OI, volume, depth, WebSocket ticks).

The human always makes the final trading decision.

There is no path: signal → broker → order.
